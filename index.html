Selected Case Studies

Physician UX for Alzheimer’s Biomarker Ordering & Communication

Phase: Development & Workflow Design

Research Goal: Understand physician decision-making, workflows, and communication strategies when ordering and explaining Alzheimer’s disease biomarker tests.

My Role: Led mixed-methods UX research, including survey design and qualitative interviews, and synthesized findings into product and workflow recommendations.

Methods: Surveys (Quantitative), in-depth qualitative interviews, workflow analysis

Approach: Used surveys to identify broad patterns and variability in physician practices, followed by interviews to understand underlying decision-making processes and communication challenges.

Key Insights: Physicians showed variability in how they interpret and communicate biomarker results, with gaps in workflow support and uncertainty in explaining risk to patients.

Impact: Findings informed improvements to tools and workflows supporting biomarker ordering and communication.


UX Lead – Avian Flu Detection Tool for Farmers

Phase: Implementation & Adoption (Ongoing)

Research Goal: Evaluate usability, adoption barriers, and facilitators for integrating a biosensor tool into farmers’ workflows.

My Role: Leading UX research to assess usability, perceived usefulness, and real-world feasibility.

Methods: Qualitative interviews (in progress), usability evaluation (planned)

Approach: Conducting interviews to understand whether the biosensor is user-friendly, how it fits into existing workflows, and what factors influence adoption in real-world agricultural settings.

Key Focus Areas: Workflow integration, usability, perceived usefulness, environmental constraints, and adoption barriers.

Impact: Research will inform design improvements to support adoption and effective real-world use.


Alzheimer’s Risk Report Usability Testing

Phase: Development, Evaluation & Implementation

Research Goal: Evaluate comprehension, psychological impact, and cultural appropriateness of biomarker risk reports.

My Role: Led mixed-methods research combining surveys, usability testing, and interviews.

Methods: Surveys, usability testing, qualitative interviews

Approach: Used surveys to assess patterns in understanding and emotional response, and interviews/usability testing to identify comprehension challenges, trust issues, and user experience barriers.

Key Insights: Users experienced challenges interpreting risk information and varied in emotional response; clarity and cultural sensitivity were critical for effective communication.

Impact: Findings informed refinements to the report and are currently supporting implementation of biomarker result return to participants.
