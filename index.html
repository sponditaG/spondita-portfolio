Selected Case Studies

Physician UX for Alzheimer’s Biomarker Ordering & Communication

Role: Lead UX Researcher

Led qualitative research with physicians to understand how they explain and order Alzheimer’s disease biomarker tests, including key decision points, workflow pain points, and mental models around diagnostic communication.

Conducted interviews and usability-oriented evaluations of tools supporting biomarker ordering and communication. Synthesized findings into recommendations that improved workflow clarity, reduced friction in test selection, and supported clearer patient-provider communication.

UX Lead – Avian Flu Detection Tool for Farmers

Role: UX Research Lead

Directed UX research for a digital avian flu detection tool designed for farmers operating in real-world, high-pressure environments. Focused on understanding user constraints, context of use, and behavioral needs that influence adoption and decision-making.

Conducted interviews and usability testing to identify barriers to use and opportunities for improvement. Delivered actionable recommendations that strengthened usability, supported faster interpretation, and improved the fit between the tool and farmers’ day-to-day workflows.

Alzheimer’s Risk Report Usability Testing

Role: UX Researcher

Led iterative usability testing of digital biomarker risk reports to identify comprehension barriers, navigation challenges, and trust-related issues. Findings informed design improvements that enhanced readability, clarity, and user confidence.
